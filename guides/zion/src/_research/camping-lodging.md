# Zion — Camping and lodging

All facts fetched live on **2026-09-17** unless marked UNVERIFIED.

## Watchman Campground
Sources:
- https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/watchman-campground.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)
- https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/campgrounds-in-zion.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)
- https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/232445 (fetched 2026-09-17)
- https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/camping-regulations.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)
- https://www.nps.gov/zion/learn/news/zion-2024-campground-and-wilderness-permit-changes.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)

- Location: just inside the **South Entrance**, next to the Zion Canyon Visitor Center and the Pedestrian Entrance;
  walking distance to Springdale.
- **Open year-round.**
- **Reservation window: "Standard sites can be reserved up to 6 months in advance"** on recreation.gov
  (or 1-877-444-6777). Recreation.gov: "Reservations are available 6 months in advance."
- **Fees (effective July 1, 2024, still current): electric $45/night; non-electric $35/night.**
  Group sites: fee not published on any NPS page fetched — UNVERIFIED.
  Dump station for non-campers: **$5**.
- Loops:
  - **A & B — standard electric sites**, "may be used for either tent or RV camping". 30-amp; "only a handful"
    of 50-amp sites (50-amp claim is from recreation.gov's own description).
  - **C & D — non-electric**; "do not permit vehicles 19 feet and over or in combined length" (verified on
    recreation.gov site page C014: "STANDARD NONELECTRIC", max 6 people, 2 vehicles, RV/trailer limit "19 ft").
  - **E — group sites**, tent-only, "can accommodate between seven and 40 people".
  - **F — walk-to, tent-only**, with shade pergolas.
- **No full-hookup sites. No water or sewer at individual sites.** Dump station with potable water at the
  campground entrance. Toilets, drinking water and picnic tables provided.
- **Generators are PROHIBITED at Watchman Campground** (they are allowed at South and Lava Point, 8–10 a.m. and 6–8 p.m. only).
- Quiet hours 10 p.m. – 8 a.m. Check-out 11:00 a.m.; check-in after 12:00 p.m.
- Standard site limit: 6 people, 2 vehicles (or 4 motorcycles; only 1 RV), 3 tents.
- Stay limits: 14 days per party March–November; 30 more days outside that window; 44 days per year total.
  Group sites: 7 days per visit, 14 days per calendar year.
- **Walk-in / first-come:** none. Neither the NPS page nor the recreation.gov listing mentions any first-come,
  first-served sites, and the park states reservations are required and campgrounds are "full almost every night
  mid-March through late November". Treat Watchman as 100% reservation.
- UNVERIFIED: total site count. NPS pages do not print it; recreation.gov lists individual sites but no total
  (fetcher counted "approximately 228" site pages, which includes non-bookable entries). A third-party source
  (thedyrt/nationalparkstraveler style) says 176 sites incl. 7 ADA and 6 group — NOT used as fact in the guide.
  Guide says "sites in five loops (A–F)" and "varies" rather than inventing a count.

## South Campground
- **Reopened Friday, May 29, 2026** after rehabilitation; reservations required through recreation.gov.
  "within walking distance of the Zion Canyon Visitor Center, the Zion Canyon Shuttle System, and the town of Springdale."
  "expected to remain open through summer and fall before closing for the winter season."
  — https://www.nps.gov/zion/learn/news/zion-national-park-to-reopen-south-campground-on-may-29.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)
- **Reservation window: up to 14 days before arrival** (NPS camping page: "up to two weeks in advance").
  — https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/campgrounds-in-zion.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)
- **Fee: $35/night non-electric** (2024 fee change, still current).
  — https://www.nps.gov/zion/learn/news/zion-2024-campground-and-wilderness-permit-changes.htm
- Closes seasonally in winter. Generators allowed 8–10 a.m. and 6–8 p.m.
- UNVERIFIED: site count. A news search returned "117 available camp sites" and "$35 tent / $50 group" from
  secondary outlets; not on an NPS page, so not stated as fact in the guide.
- STATUS ANSWER for the brief: South Campground is **REOPENED as of May 29, 2026** — it is no longer closed for rehab.

## Lava Point Campground
- **"six primitive campsites"** on Kolob Terrace Road, "approximately 1 hour from Zion Canyon".
  "Sites can be reserved up to 14 days in advance" on recreation.gov.
  Amenities per campground map: pit/vault toilets, fire rings, picnic tables, trash cans. **No water.**
  "Lava Point Campground cannot be included in the itinerary of any wilderness backpacking trip."
  Closes seasonally in winter; Lava Point Road "typically closed fall into spring".
  — https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/lava-point-campground.htm and .../conditions.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)
- **Fee: $25/night** (2024 fee change). — zion-2024-campground-and-wilderness-permit-changes.htm
- UNVERIFIED: elevation (~7,890 ft is commonly cited) and RV/trailer length limits — not on the NPS page. Omitted.

## Zion Lodge (in park)
- https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/lodging.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)
- "Located in the heart of Zion Canyon". Shuttle stop #5.
- **"76 hotel rooms, 6 suites, and 40 historic cabins."**
- **"It is the only place inside the park to purchase food."** Red Rock Grill (year-round);
  Castle Dome Café and The Beer Garden (seasonal, spring–fall). Gift shop and post office.
- Reservations: 1-833-778-9290 / https://www.zionlodge.com/
- Lodge guests with reservations may drive the Zion Canyon Scenic Drive to designated parking even in shuttle season.
- Narrated tram rides through Zion Canyon run spring through fall from the Lodge.
- UNVERIFIED: the "book up to 13 months in advance, starting on the 1st of the month" booking window comes from
  third-party guides (myutahparks / allzion), not from nps.gov or zionlodge.com directly. Guide states it as
  "roughly a year ahead" and points at zionlodge.com — FLAGGED.
- Note: the concession transitioned to a new operator (Destination Zion Lodge) on January 1, 2025 per an NPS news release.

## Springdale, UT
- Sits at the South Entrance; the park's mailing address is "1 Zion Park Blvd., Springdale, UT 84767" and the
  South Entrance is "located on the north end of Springdale".
  — https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/directions.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)
- **Free Springdale town shuttle: 9 stops, free, "picks up and drops off visitors at the Zion National Park
  Pedestrian/Bike Entrance"**, every 10–15 minutes, running the same season as the park shuttle
  (8 a.m.–7 p.m.; to 8 p.m. May 17–Sep 12).
  — https://www.springdale.utah.gov/424/Shuttles and the NPS shuttle page (fetched 2026-09-17)
- "Springdale offers parking for a fee" on streets and in lots, including large-vehicle parking; "a free shuttle
  runs during the summer season, allowing visitors to park and ride to the Pedestrian Entrance."
  — https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/traffic.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)
- Zion Canyon Visitors Bureau: "Hotels, motels, Inns, bed and breakfasts and campgrounds accommodate a variety of
  tastes and budgets." — https://www.zionpark.com/ (fetched 2026-09-17)
- UNVERIFIED: no official source names individual Springdale hotels or gives a property count. The guide names a few
  long-established, well-known properties (Cliffrose, Desert Pearl Inn, Cable Mountain Lodge, Flanigan's, Bumbleberry
  Inn, Hotel DeNovo) as examples only, with no prices. Hotel DeNovo is confirmed as Springdale shuttle stop 9 and
  Zion Canyon Village as stop 1 by the NPS shuttle page. FLAGGED as editorial.

## Nearby towns (budget alternatives)
- **Hurricane / La Verkin**, UT — on UT-9 between I-15 and the park; the standard cheaper base.
- **St. George**, UT — 49 miles from the park (St. George Regional Airport SGU distance, per NPS directions page).
- **Kanab**, UT — east side, on the way to the North Rim / Bryce.
- **Cedar City**, UT — 60 miles (Cedar City Regional Airport distance, per NPS directions page);
  17 miles north of the Kolob Canyons entrance.
- UNVERIFIED: exact driving distances from Hurricane/La Verkin and Kanab to the South Entrance are not published
  on nps.gov. The guide describes them qualitatively and uses only the airport-distance figures NPS prints.
