# Zion — Permits, lotteries and the shuttle

All facts fetched live on **2026-09-17** unless marked UNVERIFIED.

## Do you need a reservation to enter Zion? NO.
- "You do not need a permit or reservation to enter the park"
- "You do not need a permit or reservation to ride a park shuttle"
- "All park visitors are required to purchase a recreational use pass upon entering"
  — https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/index.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)

## Angels Landing permit
Source: https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/angels-landing-hiking-permits.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)
and https://www.nps.gov/zion/learn/news/2026-02-11-angels-landing.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)

- "Everyone who hikes Angels Landing needs to have a permit." Required past **Scout Lookout**, on the chain section.
- "The start time for your hike is at the Grotto." Rangers may request permit verification anywhere on the trail.
- Lottery site: https://www.recreation.gov/permits/4675325
- **Fees: $6 application (covers up to 6 people, non-refundable) + $3 per person if awarded.**
  Per-person fee refundable if canceled 2+ days before the hike (seasonal lottery).
- Maximum **6 people per application**.

### Day-before lottery
- "You can apply for a permit the day before your planned hike. This lottery opens every day at 12:01 a.m. and
  closes at 3 p.m. Mountain Time (MT)." Permits issued at **4 p.m. MT the same day**.
- Same $6 application + $3 per person fees.
- Seasonal lottery applications "allow visitors to select flexible date ranges or specific hike dates and start times."

## The Narrows permits
Source: https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/narrowspermits.htm and
https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/wildernesspermitinfo.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)

- **Bottom-up from Temple of Sinawava: NO permit** (upstream as far as Big Spring).
- **Top-down day hike (16 mi from Chamberlain's Ranch): wilderness permit required.** Three ways to get one:
  1. Advance reservation on recreation.gov "at least 1 day in advance"
  2. Daily lottery — "only available two days before" the trip; apply 12:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m. MT; results ~4:00 p.m. MT
  3. First-come, first-served at the Zion Canyon Visitor Center Wilderness Desk "the day before a trip date"
- **Overnight in the Narrows: wilderness permit for your campsite.** Advance reservation at least 1 day ahead, or
  walk-in the day before / early on the day of the trip.
- Visitors "must arrange their own transportation to the Chamberlain's Ranch trailhead"
  (https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/thenarrows.htm).

### Wilderness permit fees (effective Jan 5, 2024; still current 2026-09-17)
- Day use: **$6 non-refundable application/reservation fee + $10 per person per day (refundable)**. Walk-up: no application fee.
- Overnight: **$20 non-refundable reservation fee + $7 per person per night (refundable)**. Walk-up booking also $20.
  — https://www.nps.gov/zion/learn/news/zion-2024-campground-and-wilderness-permit-changes.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)
- Permits required for "all overnight backpacking trips, overnight climbing bivouacs, all through-hikes of The Virgin
  River Narrows and its tributaries, all canyons requiring the use of descending gear or ropes, and all trips into
  Left Fork of North Creek".
- Permits are picked up in person at the Zion Canyon Visitor Center Wilderness Desk ("allow at least 20 minutes").
  "The individual listed as the reservation holder must be the one to obtain the permit." Non-transferable.

## Canyoneering permits
Source: https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/canyoneeringpermits.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)
- Seasonal lottery (Left Fork/Subway and Mystery Canyon only): applications open "two months before each
  three-month hiking season"; pick "up to four dates"; results sent on the **27th**; winners claim within two weeks;
  unclaimed spots released on the **15th of the following month at 10:00 a.m. MT**.
- Advance reservations for other canyons: "up to three months in advance on a seasonal basis", bookable until
  "5:00 pm MT, the day before your trip".
- Daily lottery: one application per person per day; apply "two days before your desired hike date, between
  12:00 am and 3:00 pm MT"; drawing at **4:00 p.m. MT**.
- Walk-in permits the day before at the Visitor Center Wilderness Desk, if spaces remain.
- Fees: $6 non-refundable application; $10 per person (refundable).
- CONFLICT: the Subway page gives the daily-lottery drawing as "3 pm MT"; the canyoneering-permits page says 4:00 pm MT.
  Guide uses "results the same afternoon" language to avoid picking a wrong minute. FLAGGED.

## The Subway (Left Fork of North Creek) — alsoConsider only
Source: https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/thesubway.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)
- "A Wilderness permit is always required for this area." Maximum group size **12 people**.
- Bottom-up (hiking): "9-mile round-trip", "very strenuous", "ankle to knee deep water", "scrambling over large boulders".
  Starts and ends at the Left Fork Trailhead on Kolob Terrace Road.
- Top-down (canyoneering): "very strenuous 9.5-mile through-hike" from Wildcat Canyon to Left Fork Trailhead;
  "requires rappelling skills, 60 feet of rope" and "swimming through several deep pools".

## Zion Canyon shuttle — 2026
Sources: https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/zion-canyon-shuttle-system.htm and
https://www.nps.gov/zion/learn/news/2026-02-06-shuttle-schedule.htm (both fetched 2026-09-17)

- **"Zion National Park's shuttle service begins on Saturday, March 7, 2026."**
  Season runs **March 7 – November 28, 2026**, then a holiday run **December 26, 2026 – January 2, 2027**.
- Shuttles are **free**; no ticket or reservation. Park entrance fee still applies.

### Scenic Drive and private cars
- "During the shuttle season, visitors cannot drive personal vehicles on the Zion Canyon Scenic Drive."
  Exception: "Only Zion Lodge and Canyon Trail Rides guests with reservations are allowed up the Zion Canyon Scenic
  Drive to designated parking areas," plus medical accommodation permits.
- Private vehicles may drive the Scenic Drive only outside shuttle season — in 2026 that means roughly
  **Nov 29 – Dec 25, 2026 and Jan 3 – early March 2027**.
- "Zion Canyon Scenic Drive will close for the day when there are no more legal parking spaces – often in
  December, January, and February." — https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/traffic.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)

### Shuttle rules
- Wheelchairs: all buses accessible; lift limit 600 lbs combined; chairs no larger than 45" long / 25" wide.
- Pets: "Not allowed on the shuttle" except ADA service dogs.
- Bicycles: allowed, self-loaded on the front rack. "No fat tire or e-bikes will be allowed."
