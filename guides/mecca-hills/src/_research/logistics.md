# Logistics — getting there, services, fees, permits

Fetch date for every entry: **2026-09-17**. Everyone on this trip drives from the Huntington Beach /
LA-OC area. No flights, no rental cars — nothing in this guide should mention them.

## 1. The route from Huntington Beach

**What is officially sourced:**
- BLM: Mecca Hills Wilderness is in "Riverside County; **15 miles southeast of Indio, California**."
- BLM: "Access this wilderness **from the east from State Highway 195 (Box Canyon Road) via Interstate
  10** or **from the south at Mecca via State Highway 111**. Painted Canyon Road provides four-wheel
  drive access at the intersection of State Highway 195 and the Coachella Canal."
  — https://www.blm.gov/visit/mecca-hills-wilderness (fetched 2026-09-17)
- DesertUSA fills in the turn in town: "When you reach Mecca veer right on **66th Ave.**, which is also
  State Highway 195. The road will curve toward the left and become **Box Canyon Road**," then Painted
  Canyon Road.
  — https://www.desertusa.com/desert-california/painted-canyon-mecca.html (fetched 2026-09-17)
- Naming caveat: BLM still calls Box Canyon Road "State Highway 195." The eastern segment lost its
  state designation in **1972**, and SR 195 was deleted entirely in **2014**; it is a Riverside County
  road today, and signs say Box Canyon Road, not 195.
  — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_Route_195 (fetched 2026-09-17)

**The route the guide gives** (described, not measured): CA-39 or Beach Blvd to **CA-91 east**, CA-91
to **I-215 / CA-60 or I-10 east** through the pass to **Indio**, then **CA-86 south** (or CA-111 south
through Coachella) to **Mecca**, **66th Avenue east** out of Mecca onto **Box Canyon Road**, then left
onto **Painted Canyon Road** and about 4 miles of dirt north to the trailhead.

**Drive time: UNVERIFIED.** I found **no official or citable source** for the distance or driving time
from Huntington Beach to Mecca. The guide says "**roughly 2½ to 3 hours in normal traffic**" and labels
it as approximate, which matches the host's own framing ("2–3 hours from the beach"). No mileage figure
is stated anywhere in the data. If someone wants a number later, measure it and cite the measurement.

**One sourced sub-leg to anchor it:** 15 miles from Indio to the wilderness (BLM, above), and Salton
Sea SRA is "30 miles south of Indio on Highway 111" (California State Parks), which brackets Mecca.
— https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=639 (fetched 2026-09-17)

## 2. Fees and permits

- **Mecca Hills Wilderness / Painted Canyon: no fee.** BLM's page lists "Fees: N/A." No entrance
  station, no day-use fee, no camping fee, no reservation.
  — https://www.blm.gov/visit/mecca-hills-wilderness (fetched 2026-09-17)
- **No hiking permit and no camping permit** are published for this unit. Dispersed camping on BLM land
  generally needs no permit; the national camping page notes permits are needed for large organized
  groups or in some areas for campfires.
  — https://www.blm.gov/programs/recreation/camping (fetched 2026-09-17)
- **California Campfire Permit — required, free, and the one thing to get in advance.** Required
  year-round to use a campfire, barbecue or **gas stove** on BLM land outside a developed campground.
  Free, valid for the calendar year, available at readyforwildfire.org and at any BLM, Forest Service
  or CAL FIRE office.
  — https://www.blm.gov/programs/fire/regional-info/california/fire-restrictions
  — https://www.readyforwildfire.org/permits/ (both fetched 2026-09-17)
- **Joshua Tree National Park (only if the group drives to Cottonwood Spring): $30 per private vehicle,
  7 days**; $25 motorcycle; $15 per person on foot or bike (under 16 free); $55 park annual pass.
  Entrance stations take **card only**.
  — https://www.nps.gov/jotr/planyourvisit/fees.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)
- **Salton Sea State Recreation Area: $7 day use per vehicle** ($5 seniors 62+).
  — https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=639 (fetched 2026-09-17)
- **Coachella Valley Preserve / Thousand Palms Oasis: no entrance fee**, donations welcome.
  — https://www.cnlm.org/portfolio_page/thousand-palms-oasis-preserve-coachella-valley-preserve/ (fetched 2026-09-17)

## 3. Services — fuel, water, groceries

**UNVERIFIED in detail.** I could not find an official source listing businesses in Mecca. What is
solid:
- There is **no water, no store, no fuel and no ranger station** anywhere in the Mecca Hills Wilderness
  or on Painted Canyon Road. BLM lists no visitor facilities for the unit and tells visitors to bring
  their own water.
  — https://www.blm.gov/visit/mecca-hills-wilderness (fetched 2026-09-17)
- The nearest staffed public facility is the **BLM Palm Springs–South Coast Field Office**, 1201 Bird
  Center Drive, Palm Springs, 760-833-7100, public room **8 a.m.–4 p.m. Monday–Friday**, closed federal
  holidays — that is over an hour away and not a trip-day resource, but it is the number to call for
  road and fire status.
  — https://www.blm.gov/office/palm-springs-south-coast-field-office (fetched 2026-09-17)
- The **Salton Sea SRA visitor center** (9 a.m.–4 p.m. daily, closed Thanksgiving and Christmas) is the
  nearest staffed public building to camp, with restrooms.
  — https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=639 (fetched 2026-09-17)

**Decision for the guide:** say plainly that Coachella and Indio, on the highway 15–25 minutes back
toward I-10, are the last full supermarkets and reliable fuel, that Mecca itself is a small town with
limited services, and **name no specific business** — I have no citable source for hours or even
continued existence. Buy everything, and fill the tank, before turning onto Box Canyon Road.

## 4. Cell service

BLM: the area has "**little to no cell service**"; download maps before arriving. Camper reports vary
by carrier (some report AT&T and T-Mobile bars near the road entrance, Verizon poor). The guide states
BLM's line and tells people to download offline maps and agree on a meeting time, not to rely on texts.
— https://www.blm.gov/visit/mecca-hills-wilderness (fetched 2026-09-17)
— https://maps.campendium.com/us/mecca-ca/camping-rv/painted-canyon-mecca-ca--4 (fetched 2026-09-17)

## 5. Getting around once there

- Everything on this trip is reached by car from camp. Inside the wilderness: **no vehicles, no
  bicycles** — "Motorized equipment and equipment used for mechanical transport are generally
  prohibited on all federal lands designated as wilderness... including the use of motor vehicles
  (including OHVs), motorboats, motorized equipment, bicycles, hang gliders, wagons, carts, portage
  wheels, and the landing of aircraft."
  — https://www.blm.gov/visit/mecca-hills-wilderness (fetched 2026-09-17)
- The seasonal fire order also prohibits motor vehicles and internal combustion engines **off
  established roads and trails** district-wide through October 29, 2026.
  — https://www.blm.gov/announcement/blm-announces-seasonal-fire-restrictions-southern-california (fetched 2026-09-17)

## 6. Nearby-town lodging (for the "I'm not camping" crowd)

Indio, Coachella and Mecca are the nearest towns on CA-86/CA-111, with chain and independent motels in
Indio in particular. **I deliberately name no properties and give no prices** — I have no official or
stable source, and this repo's rule is that every factual field traces to a URL. The guide describes
the towns and their position on the highway, cites the BLM "15 miles southeast of Indio" line for
distance, and leaves booking to the reader.

## 8. Dogs — UNVERIFIED for Mecca Hills itself

BLM publishes **no pet rule** on the Mecca Hills Wilderness page; BLM land is generally open to dogs,
and dogs are not among the prohibited items in the wilderness regulations BLM lists (which cover
motorized and mechanized transport only).
— https://www.blm.gov/visit/mecca-hills-wilderness (fetched 2026-09-17)
The guide therefore says "allowed on BLM land" without citing a specific permission, and adds the two
things that *are* sourced:
- Getting a dog up and down the ladders "would be tough" — hikingguy
  https://hikingguy.com/hiking-trails/palm-springs-hiking-trails/ladder-canyon-trail-painted-canyon-loop-hike/
- **Coachella Valley Preserve: "Dogs and other pets are not allowed," service dogs on leash excepted.**
  https://www.cnlm.org/portfolio_page/thousand-palms-oasis-preserve-coachella-valley-preserve/
(both fetched 2026-09-17)
