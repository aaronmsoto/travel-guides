# Mecca Hills — attractions research

All facts below carry a source URL and a fetch date. Anything I could not confirm against an
official (BLM / NPS / California State Parks / NOAA / USNO / USGS) source is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

Fetch date for every entry: **2026-09-17** unless stated otherwise.

---

## Framing: what agency manages this, and under what name

- Mecca Hills Wilderness: **26,242 acres**, managed by the BLM **Palm Springs–South Coast Field
  Office** (1201 Bird Center Drive, Palm Springs CA 92262; 760-833-7100). "In 1994 the Mecca Hills
  Wilderness became part of the ... National Wilderness Preservation System."
  Location given as "Riverside County; 15 miles southeast of Indio, California." **Fees: N/A.**
  Listed activities: camping, hiking, picnicking, wildlife viewing, wilderness, photography.
  — https://www.blm.gov/visit/mecca-hills-wilderness (fetched 2026-09-17)
- Designating law: **Public Law 103-433, California Desert Protection Act of 1994, October 31, 1994**;
  wilderness.net lists the original acreage as 24,200 (BLM now says 26,242 — the two figures differ;
  use BLM's).
  — https://wilderness.net/visit-wilderness/?ID=349 (fetched 2026-09-17)
- Since **January 14, 2025** the Mecca Hills Wilderness sits inside **Chuckwalla National Monument**
  (~624,270 acres of BLM-managed public land; five wilderness areas inside it — Mecca Hills,
  Orocopia Mountains, Chuckwalla Mountains, Little Chuckwalla Mountains, Palo Verde Mountains).
  — https://www.blm.gov/programs/national-conservation-lands/california/chuckwalla (fetched 2026-09-17)
  - The monument is the subject of active litigation; in **March 2026** a district court allowed nine
    conservation organizations and several Tribal Nations to intervene to defend it. No designation has
    ever been overturned. This does not change day-to-day access today.
    — https://earthjustice.org/press/2026/court-allows-organizations-to-intervene-in-lawsuit-to-defend-chuckwalla-national-monument (fetched 2026-09-17)

## BLM's own alert text (quoted in the guide)

> "If you plan to visit Painted Canyon or the Mecca Hills Wilderness, please take extra precautions as
> these areas have little to no cell service and **no designated trails**. Download your map before
> arriving and bring plenty of water, at least one liter per person, along with electrolytes. Plan to
> start your hike early, as temperatures rise quickly by early afternoon. Always hike with a buddy and
> let someone know when you expect to leave and return."
— https://www.blm.gov/visit/mecca-hills-wilderness (fetched 2026-09-17)

## BLM description of the landscape (quoted/paraphrased)

> "Thanks to the restless San Andreas Fault, the geologic formations of Mecca Hills Wilderness are
> among the most unusual sites of their kind in the world. Entire regions are exposed layers of eroded
> rock, some over 600 million years old... The area is a badlands labyrinth, a natural maze of small,
> narrow, steep canyons. Colorful Painted Canyon runs in a general north-south path through the middle
> of the Wilderness. Sandy washes sprinkled with ironwood, smoke trees, and paloverde divide the area,
> while ocotillo squat on gentler slopes and the tops of mesas. Bighorn sheep occasionally cross over
> from the Orocopia Mountains on the east... You may see spotted bats, desert tortoises, and prairie
> falcons. The non-Wilderness corridor of Box Canyon Road splits off a small southern section of the
> area in which you'll find Sheep Hole Oasis and Hidden Springs Canyon (reliable sources for water).
> If you look closely, you'll discover caves, known locally as grottoes."
— https://www.blm.gov/visit/mecca-hills-wilderness (fetched 2026-09-17)

## The only official trail-mileage statement I found

> "two marked trails offer hikes to particularly scenic spots. Painted Canyon offers a moderate hike of
> up to five miles round trip through colorful rock formations."
— https://www.recreation.gov/camping/gateways/13425 (fetched 2026-09-17)

This is the closest thing to an official distance for the Painted Canyon / Ladder Canyon outing. BLM
publishes no trail mileages, no elevation gains and no trail times for this wilderness — it says there
are *no designated trails*. Every mileage below that is not this sentence comes from secondary sources
and is flagged.

## 1. Ladder Canyon (id `ladder-canyon`) — hike

- Coordinates used: **33.6191, -115.9994** (trailhead parking at the end of Painted Canyon Road).
  Source: https://stavislost.com/hikes/trail/ladder-canyon-and-painted-canyon-loop/ (fetched 2026-09-17).
  Hikingguy gives 33.619046, -116.0015676 for the same lot — the two differ by about 700 ft.
  **UNVERIFIED-precision** (no BLM-published trailhead coordinate exists). Both land in the same
  parking area.
- USGS 3DEP elevation at 33.6191, -115.9994: **587 ft**.
  — https://epqs.nationalmap.gov/v1/json?x=-115.9994&y=33.6191&units=Feet&wkid=4326 (queried 2026-09-17)
- Distances, **CONFLICTING** across secondary sources:
  - hikingguy: **5 mi**, **560 ft** gain, ~3 hr, "moderate"
    — https://hikingguy.com/hiking-trails/palm-springs-hiking-trails/ladder-canyon-trail-painted-canyon-loop-hike/
  - Stav is Lost: **5.2 mi**, **1,100 ft** gain, 3–5 hr, Class 2
    — https://stavislost.com/hikes/trail/ladder-canyon-and-painted-canyon-loop/
  - Other secondary write-ups quote 4.3–4.7 mi and ~2.5 hr.
  - Recreation.gov (official) says "up to five miles round trip."
  **Decision:** the guide says "about 4.5–5 miles round trip" and "about 600 ft of climbing
  (sources vary)", and cites Recreation.gov for the five-mile figure. Do not present a precise number.
- Ladders: wooden/metal ladders bolted or wedged into dry falls in the slot. hikingguy: tallest is
  "probably about 12-15 feet"; "Sometimes a rung or two can be missing." **UNVERIFIED** — no official
  BLM statement about ladder count, ownership, inspection or maintenance exists. The ladders are
  **not** BLM infrastructure as far as any official page states. Recent user reports (Jan–Apr 2026 on
  AllTrails) describe them as in place and usable, with one Feb 15, 2026 report calling the last
  ladder dangerous. Treat ladder condition as **unknown until you are standing there**.
  — https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/ladder-canyon-and-painted-canyon-trail--4 (fetched 2026-09-17)
- Loop direction: ascend Ladder Canyon, descend Big Painted Canyon (hikingguy, Stav is Lost).
- Entrance to Ladder Canyon is an unmarked slot on the left of the main wash a short way up from the
  lot, often marked by a cairn. Easy to walk past. (hikingguy; Stav is Lost.)
- Dogs: "technically allowed but getting them up and down the ladders would be tough" (hikingguy).
- No cell service in the canyons (hikingguy; matches the BLM alert).

## 2. Painted Canyon / the drive in and camp area (id `painted-canyon`) — area

- BLM: "**Painted Canyon Road provides four-wheel drive access** at the intersection of State Highway
  195 and the Coachella Canal."
  — https://www.blm.gov/visit/mecca-hills-wilderness (fetched 2026-09-17)
  Note BLM still calls Box Canyon Road "State Highway 195"; that designation was removed from the
  eastern segment in 1972 and SR 195 itself was deleted in 2014 — it is a Riverside County road now.
  — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_Route_195 (fetched 2026-09-17)
- Road length, **CONFLICTING**: DesertUSA says "well-maintained, graded road (approx. 4 miles)" and
  elsewhere "approximately 3 miles until you enter the canyon"
  (https://www.desertusa.com/desert-california/painted-canyon-mecca.html); hikingguy says "about 4.5
  miles on the unpaved Painted Canyon Road"; freecampsites says "approximately 4 miles."
  **Decision:** "about 4 miles of dirt" with "about 4–5 miles" as the honest range.
- Coordinates used for the camp/road corridor: **33.6155, -116.0000**, roughly the last mile of
  Painted Canyon Road. **UNVERIFIED-precision** (a road corridor, not a point). USGS 3DEP around
  600 ft there.
- Camping detail is in `access-road-camping.md`.

## 3. Big Painted Canyon (id `big-painted-canyon`) — canyon

- The wide, red- and ochre-walled main wash that the loop descends after the ridge. Two short side
  canyons branch off it during the descent, "with one featuring a ladder to access it."
  — https://stavislost.com/hikes/trail/ladder-canyon-and-painted-canyon-loop/ (fetched 2026-09-17)
- DesertUSA: "A sandy wash runs the length of the main canyon (**1.7 miles**), with a few narrow side
  canyons which can be explored further on foot." **UNVERIFIED** against BLM.
  — https://www.desertusa.com/desert-california/painted-canyon-mecca.html (fetched 2026-09-17)
- Coordinates used: **33.6230, -115.9960** (upper wash, on the loop's return leg). USGS 3DEP: 822 ft.
  **UNVERIFIED-precision** — chosen from the topography, not from a published waypoint.

## 4. The ridge above Ladder Canyon (id `ladder-canyon-overlook`) — viewpoint

- hikingguy: "At the top of the hill, the views open up and it's spectacular," with San Jacinto and
  San Gorgonio visible. The Salton Sea is visible from ridges in the Mecca Hills (Friends of the
  Desert Mountains describes the Grottos route as following "a ridge offering Salton Sea views").
  — https://hikingguy.com/hiking-trails/palm-springs-hiking-trails/ladder-canyon-trail-painted-canyon-loop-hike/
  — https://www.desertmountains.org/event/grottos-trail-hike/ (both fetched 2026-09-17)
- Coordinates used: **33.6245, -116.0075**. USGS 3DEP samples nearby give 862–965 ft, i.e. roughly
  **300–400 ft above the trailhead**. **UNVERIFIED-precision** — derived from elevation probing, not
  from a published waypoint.
  — https://epqs.nationalmap.gov/v1/json (queried 2026-09-17)

## 5. Salton Sea State Recreation Area (id `salton-sea`) — area

- Address 100-225 State Park Road, Mecca, CA 92254; "30 miles south of Indio on Highway 111."
- **Day use $7.00 per vehicle** ($5.00 seniors 62+). Camping: tent $20, hookups $30, primitive $10,
  group $75 per night.
- Visitor center open year round, **9 a.m.–4 p.m.**, closed Thanksgiving and Christmas.
- **Current closures: Mecca Beach Campground closed for maintenance repairs; Bombay Beach Campground
  closed; Varner Harbor closed to vessel access until further notice** (declining water levels).
  — https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=639 (fetched 2026-09-17)
- Coordinates used: **33.5085, -115.9173** (Varner Harbor / park headquarters area). USGS 3DEP there:
  **-217 ft**, consistent with a below-sea-level shoreline. **UNVERIFIED-precision**.
- Air quality and dust from the shrinking shoreline are a known regional issue; I found no official
  advisory page to cite for our dates, so the guide says only that the shoreline smells of sulfur and
  decaying fish at times — **UNVERIFIED**, kept out of the data as a claim with numbers.

## 6. Box Canyon Road (id `box-canyon-road`) — scenic-drive

- BLM: the non-wilderness Box Canyon Road corridor splits the southern part of the wilderness; it is
  the access "from the east ... via Interstate 10."
  — https://www.blm.gov/visit/mecca-hills-wilderness (fetched 2026-09-17)
- Length, **CONFLICTING**: secondary sources give "approximately 15.8 miles", "17 miles" and
  "20 miles" between I-10/Cottonwood Springs Road and Mecca. Paved, two lanes.
  — https://www.gribblenation.org/2019/10/box-canyon-road-former-us-60-us-70-and.html and
    https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g32700-d7648973-Reviews-Box_Canyon_Road-Mecca_California.html
    (fetched 2026-09-17)
  **Decision:** the guide says "about 16 miles" is the shortest sourced figure and describes it as
  "roughly 15–20 miles depending on where you start it" — flagged approximate.
- History: this was once US 60 / US 70 and the second SR 195; the state gave the eastern segment up in
  1972. — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_Route_195 (fetched 2026-09-17)
- The road has washed out before: a **September 30, 2018** storm washed it out, October 12–13 storms
  did more damage, and a **$3 million repair finished May 3, 2019**.
  — https://ukenreport.com/box-canyon-road-reopens/ (fetched 2026-09-17)
- Coordinates used: **33.6155, -115.9080**, a point in the canyon section (USGS 3DEP 1,071 ft).
  **UNVERIFIED-precision**.

## 7. Cottonwood Spring, Joshua Tree NP (id `cottonwood-spring`) — walk

- "A lush desert oasis located in the southern portion of the Joshua Tree National Park," near the
  Cottonwood Visitor Center, "a short walk down a dirt path from the parking area." It is the trailhead
  for the Mastodon Peak Loop and the Lost Palms Oasis Trail. Facilities: parking, information kiosk,
  trailhead, hiker registration, trash.
  — https://www.nps.gov/places/cottonwood-spring.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)
- **Entrance fee: $30 per private vehicle, 7 days**; $25 motorcycle; $15 per person on foot/bike (16+,
  under 16 free); $55 Joshua Tree annual pass. Buy at the Cottonwood Visitor Center (South Entrance),
  other visitor centers, entrance stations or recreation.gov. Entrance stations take card only.
  — https://www.nps.gov/jotr/planyourvisit/fees.htm (fetched 2026-09-17)
- Coordinates used: **33.7373, -115.8105** (USGS 3DEP 3,011 ft, consistent with the Cottonwood area).
  **UNVERIFIED-precision** — NPS publishes no coordinate on that page.

## 8. Coachella Valley Preserve / Thousand Palms Oasis (id `coachella-valley-preserve`) — walk

- 29200 Thousand Palms Canyon Road, Thousand Palms, CA 92276. **880 acres**, part of the Coachella
  Valley Preserve system, managed by the Center for Natural Lands Management.
- **Hours: open Saturdays and Sundays 7 a.m.–3 p.m. from May 1 through October 31** (the Pushawalla
  and Willis Palms trails are open daily). So it **is open on Sunday October 18, 2026**.
- **No entrance fee**; donations welcome. Bathrooms available during operating hours. The visitors'
  center is **permanently closed**. Street parking only. **Dogs and other pets are not allowed**
  (service dogs on leash excepted).
- Protects the Coachella Valley fringe-toed lizard, desert pupfish, Swainson's hawk and Coachella
  Valley milkvetch.
  — https://www.cnlm.org/portfolio_page/thousand-palms-oasis-preserve-coachella-valley-preserve/ (fetched 2026-09-17)
- McCallum Trail: "1.9 mi, 111 ft of gain, about 0.5–1 hr" per AllTrails — **UNVERIFIED** against the
  managing organization. The guide says "about two miles" and flags it.
  — https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/mccallum-trail (fetched 2026-09-17)
- Coordinates used: **33.8248, -116.3072** (USGS 3DEP 569 ft). **UNVERIFIED-precision**.

## 9. The Grottos (id `the-grottos`) — hike

- BLM confirms the feature exists and names it: in the Box Canyon Road corridor "you'll find Sheep Hole
  Oasis and Hidden Springs Canyon (reliable sources for water). If you look closely, you'll discover
  caves, known locally as grottoes."
  — https://www.blm.gov/visit/mecca-hills-wilderness (fetched 2026-09-17)
- Friends of the Desert Mountains (a long-running local conservancy that guides this hike): "The
  Grottos Trail via Sheep Hole Oasis and Hidden Springs Oasis," **5.75 miles, over 800 ft of gain,
  strenuous**, "an uneven and steep path." Meetup point on Box Canyon Road at
  **33.589100, -115.930060**. Route follows a ridge with Salton Sea views, drops to a sandy wash into
  Grotto Canyon, and visits a palm grove at Hidden Springs. **Dogs not permitted** on their outings.
  — https://www.desertmountains.org/event/grottos-trail-hike/ (fetched 2026-09-17)
- **IMPORTANT / UNVERIFIED:** the same page states "The original Mecca Grotto has collapsed, so hikers
  explore the towering canyon where the Grotto entrance previously existed." No BLM page confirms or
  dates the collapse. The guide states this as a report from Friends of the Desert Mountains, not as
  BLM fact, and tells readers not to count on entering a cave.
- USGS 3DEP at the trailhead coordinate: 672 ft.
- Scrambling, boulder piles, narrow passages and occasional headlamp use are described by secondary
  write-ups. — https://hikeeveryday.blogspot.com/2009/11/grottos-mecca-hills-wilderness-area.html
  (fetched 2026-09-17) — old (2009), used only for the character of the route.

## 10. Night sky at camp (id `night-sky-at-camp`) — area

- Not a BLM-designated dark-sky site; there is **no** International Dark-Sky designation for Mecca
  Hills that I could find. The guide does not claim one. **UNVERIFIED** if anyone says otherwise.
- Moon for the night of **Saturday October 17, 2026** at 33.619, -116.000 (US Naval Observatory,
  tz -7): sunset **18:08**, end of civil twilight **18:33**, moon **42% illuminated** waxing crescent,
  **moonrise 13:24, moonset 23:14**. First Quarter falls on October 18 at 09:12. So the moon is out
  over dinner and **sets at about 11:15 p.m.**, leaving the darkest sky late.
  — https://aa.usno.navy.mil/api/rstt/oneday?date=2026-10-17&coords=33.6190,-116.0000&tz=-7
    (queried 2026-09-17)
- Coordinates used: same as camp, **33.6155, -116.0000**.

## Considered and placed in `alsoConsider` instead of the Top 10

- **Dos Palmas Preserve** — 1,400-acre BLM Area of Critical Environmental Concern with fan palms,
  springs and wetlands near the Salton Sea; access via Parkside Drive off Highway 111, parking lot with
  bathrooms at the gate. — https://www.blm.gov/visit/dos-palmas-preserve (fetched 2026-09-17)
- **Sheep Hole Oasis and Hidden Springs Canyon** — named by BLM as reliable water sources in the Box
  Canyon Road corridor (same BLM page). Reaching them is the first part of the Grottos route.
- **Ropes Canyon / Never Ending Canyon** — side canyons off Painted Canyon named in camper and hiker
  reports; **UNVERIFIED** against BLM, described without numbers.
  — https://maps.campendium.com/us/mecca-ca/camping-rv/painted-canyon-mecca-ca--4 (fetched 2026-09-17)
- **Salvation Mountain / Bombay Beach** — dropped: too far south for a one-night trip and no official
  source for hours or access.
