## Furnace Creek Campground  (NPS campgroundID 2164)
- 136 sites total; 45 tent-only; **18 full-hookup sites** (30/50-amp electric + water + sewer).
- Elevation: 190 ft below sea level. Open **year-round**.
- **Reservable season: October 15 – April 15**, via recreation.gov or 877-444-6777.
  Booking window: **5 days to 6 months in advance**. "In recent years, recreation.gov reservations
  have booked Furnace Creek Campground to capacity most days."
- **Rest of the year (late April – early October): campground reduces to 41 sites and is
  first-come, first-served.** Some loops close in summer.
- Fees (NPS feed): Standard RV/Tent **$30.00**; **Full Hook-up $44.00** ($30 standard + $14 utility fee,
  utility fee NOT discounted; standard portion half-price for Senior/Access pass holders);
  Group sites #3,4,5 **$40.00** (9–15 people, up to 4 vehicles); Group sites #1,2 **$60.00** (9–40 people, up to 10 vehicles).
  No group-site discounts.
- Amenities: potable water, flush toilets (year round), dump station, camp store, trash/recycling,
  seasonal amphitheater, seasonal host. **No showers** — showers/laundry for a fee at the adjacent
  privately run Furnace Creek Ranch (The Ranch at Death Valley).
- recreation.gov site breakdown (fetched 2026-09-17, https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/232496):
  9 full-hookup pull-through, 9 full-hookup back-in, 11 tent-only drive-in, 36 tent-only walk-in, 4 group tent walk-in.
- **EV warning (NPS):** "Electric Vehicle/EV charging is NOT supported in Furnace Creek Campground.
  Plugging your EV into these pedestals could permanently damage your vehicle and the campground's
  electrical system." EV charging is at the Oasis Ranch and Inn instead.
- 14-day annual camping limit (source: camping-in-death-valley.htm, fetched 2026-09-17).
- NPS reserves the right to administratively hold sites in all campgrounds.
- UNVERIFIED: exact check-in/check-out times and max-stay-per-reservation on recreation.gov (not shown on fetched page).

## Sunset Campground (4486)
- 230 sites, **all first-come, first-served**. Fee **$18.00** standard / **$9.00** Senior & Access pass.
- Season: "typically open late fall through mid-to-late April."
- RV max 60 ft, trailer max 50 ft. No hookups.
- Desert gravel lot, little vegetation. **Sites do NOT have fire grates or picnic tables**;
  campfires only at a few established community fire grates.
- Potable water (seasonal), flush toilets (seasonal), dump station (seasonal), camp store.
- "Almost always has sites available, even in peak season" / "rarely fills."
- Location: gravel road across from the Timbisha Shoshone village.
- Pay at automated fee machine in campground (card only) or at the visitor center across the street.

## Texas Springs Campground (4485)
- 106 sites, **all first-come, first-served**. Fee **$20.00** standard / **$10.00** Senior & Access.
- Season: "typically open from late fall through mid-April."
- RV max 35 ft, trailer max 25 ft. 26 tent-only sites. No hookups. **No generators allowed.**
- Each site has a fire grate and picnic table. In the hills above Furnace Creek; some trees, good views.
- Potable water (seasonal), flush toilets (seasonal), seasonal dump station.
- Occupy a site first, then pay at the automated fee machine.

## Stovepipe Wells Campground (4487)
- 190 sites, **all first-come, first-served**. Fee **$18.00** / **$9.00** Senior & Access.
- Season: "Open from late fall through mid-to-late April. Closed seasonally during extreme heat."
- At sea level, on CA-190, **24 miles (39 km) west of Furnace Creek**. 28 tent-only sites. No hookups.
- Potable water (seasonal), flush toilets (seasonal), dump station, camp store, firewood and ice for sale.
- Adjacent to Stovepipe Wells general store and ranger station; a separate **concession-run Stovepipe Wells
  RV Park** has hookup sites. Campers may buy pool/shower passes at the Village and use its free Wi-Fi.

## Mesquite Spring Campground (6696)
- 40 sites, **all first-come, first-served**. Fee **$20.00** / **$10.00** Senior & Access.
- **Open year-round.** Elevation 1,800 ft. NPS caution: "summer months may be HOT, with nightly lows in the 90s Fahrenheit."
- 2 miles off Scotty's Castle Road below Grapevine Canyon; 33 miles north of the CA-190 junction, then 2 miles in.
- Each site has a fire grate and picnic table. No hookups. Potable water, flush toilets (year round), dump station.
- **Access note (NPS feed):** "Access is closed until further notice if traveling from Nevada through
  Grapevine Canyon on Nevada Highway 267." Approach from CA-190 instead.

## Emigrant Campground (4488) — CLOSED
- 10 tent-only sites, free, elevation 2,100 ft, on CA-190 ~10 miles SW of Stovepipe Wells.
- **NPS: "The campground, water system, and restrooms at Emigrant have been closed since late August 2025
  due to flood damage... there is no estimated repair date."** Operating hours field: "Closed indefinitely
  due to storm damage to the water system." Fetched 2026-09-17.

## Wildrose Campground (4489)
- 23 sites, first-come first-served, **free**. Elevation 4,100 ft, Panamint Mountains, on Emigrant Canyon Road.
- **No water** ("Closest available water ~31 miles north at Stovepipe Wells"). Vault toilets year-round.
- RV max 25 ft, trailer max 10 ft. Generators allowed 7 am – 7 pm. Prone to high winds. Open year-round.
- Conditions page caution: Lower Wildrose Road — "Vehicles longer than 25ft are not allowed... Expect rough gravel conditions."

## Thorndike Campground (6705) — primitive
- 6 sites, first-come first-served, **free**. Elevation **7,400 ft**. No water, seasonal vault toilets.
- "Accessible to high clearance vehicles (no longer than 25') and 4x4 may be necessary."
- Season: "typically open from mid-April through December, pending snowfall."

## Mahogany Flat Campground (6706) — primitive
- 9 sites, first-come first-served, **free**. Elevation **8,200 ft**, pinyon-juniper forest.
- **At the Telescope Peak trailhead.** No water, seasonal vault toilets. RV max 25 ft.
- "Dirt road access requires high-clearance vehicles, often 4x4 required."
- "Suitable for summer and fall camping. Often closed in the winter due to snowy/icy conditions."

## Other primitive campgrounds in the feed (all free, first-come, high-clearance)
- Eureka Dunes (7778): 7 sites, 2,880 ft, vault toilets, no water, high-clearance only.
- Homestake Dry Camp (7779): 10 sites, 3,785 ft, near the Racetrack, **no toilets**, no water,
  "high-clearance vehicles with all-terrain tires"; Lippincott Pass for experienced 4WD only.
- Saline Valley (7781): 15 sites, 1,375 ft, clothing-optional hot springs ~35 miles from pavement,
  high-clearance + all-terrain tires, requires North Pass (7,300 ft) or South Pass (5,997 ft).

## Backcountry & roadside camping
Source: https://www.nps.gov/deva/planyourvisit/backcamp.htm — fetched 2026-09-17
- Free dispersed roadside camping is allowed along **authorized dirt roads only**, at least **1 mile
  down the road from the junction with any paved road**, in previously disturbed areas.
- Campsites must be **more than 100 yards from any water source**. Cat-holes ≥200 yards from water or camp.
- **Prohibited:** along paved roads, pullouts or parking lots; on the valley floor from Ashford Mill south
  to 2 miles north of Stovepipe Wells; Eureka Dunes; Greenwater Canyon; historic mining areas
  (Keane Wonder Mine, Ubehebe Lead Mine, Skidoo Mill); and on day-use-only roads — Titus Canyon,
  Mosaic Canyon, West Side Road, Wildrose Road, Skidoo Road, Aguereberry Point Road, first 8 miles of
  Cottonwood Canyon Road, Grotto Canyon Road, Racetrack Road, Natural Bridge Canyon, Desolation Canyon,
  Pinion Mesa Road, Big Pine Road.
- A **free permit is recommended** (not required) for all other roadside camping, backpacking,
  backcountry cabins and canyoneering — from Furnace Creek VC, Stovepipe Wells Ranger Station, or drop box.

### RESERVABLE designated roadside sites (mandatory permit, paid)
Sources: https://www.nps.gov/deva/planyourvisit/backcamp.htm and
https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/10303151 — both fetched 2026-09-17
- Facility: **"Death Valley Backcountry Roadside Camping"** on recreation.gov, **47 designated sites**:
  - **Echo Canyon Road — 9 sites** (E1–E9)
  - Hole in the Wall Road — 6 sites
  - Greenwater Valley Road — 19 sites
  - Cottonwood Canyon Road + Marble Canyon Road — 13 sites combined
- **Fee: $10 per night.** Reservable **online up to 6 months in advance**. Reservation required —
  no walk-up; permits for these designated sites are no longer issued in person.
- Echo Canyon Road begins **~4.6 miles east of Furnace Creek Visitor Center off CA-190**; Site E1 is
  1 mile down the unimproved gravel road.
- Vehicle standard (recreation.gov): **"High clearance vehicles are defined as a SUV or truck with at
  least 15 inch tire rims AND at least 8 inches of ground clearance from the lowest point of the frame,
  body, or suspension."** 4WD "highly encouraged." Trailers, RVs and passenger cars cannot reach most sites.
  Lower Echo Canyon needs moderate clearance at minimum; sites further in need high clearance + 4WD.
- Sites are spaced at least 1/2 mile apart; primitive gravel pad, no shade, no water, no toilets.
  **Generators are not allowed in the backcountry.** Off-road driving prohibited parkwide.
- Site E1 capacity: up to 12 people and 3 vehicles; 70 ft pull-through gravel driveway.
- Open year-round; recreation.gov warns temperatures "frequently range above 100F (38C) May through September."
- SUPERSEDED / conflicting: https://www.nps.gov/deva/learn/news/echo-canyon.htm (Nov 9, 2022) says the
  permit is **free**, in-person only, and "cannot be reserved in advance," for up to 7 nights. That news
  release predates the April 30, 2024 recreation.gov system. The $10/night reservable system on
  backcamp.htm + recreation.gov is the current one and is what the guide uses.
- UNVERIFIED: maximum stay length under the current paid system (7 nights under the old free permit;
  not restated on the pages fetched).
